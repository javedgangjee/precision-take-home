# 3D

## Overview
- Rule would go up to NxNxN
- (value-1) mod N^3
- I figured out 2D, by looking at N=10. A 2 digit number 98 would mean 9 in x and 7 in y. 198 would also fall in 9x7
- In 3d, that extends to the 3rd digit. So 987 would mean 9 in x, 8 in y and 7 in z.

## View
- A cube of cubes using a library like three.js
- Cubes with no hits would not be drawn
- The colour range could be a slider where only cells of the selected colour would be isolated on the screen.
- Separate sliders per axis could slice the cube for cross sectional views.
- Standard 3d view with pan, zoom and orbit would also be needed.
