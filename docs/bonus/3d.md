# 3D

## Overview
- This would be an expansion of the 2D NxN rule to NxNxN
- Formula would change to `i = (value-1) % N^3` with each axis being:
    - `x = i % N`
    - `y = i / N % N`
    - `z = i / N^2`
- In 2D, if N=10. A 2 digit number 98 would mean 7 in x and 9 in y. 198 would also fall in 7x9
- In 3D, that extends to the 3rd digit. So 987 would mean 6 in x, 8 in y and 9 in z.

## View
- A cube of cubes using a library like Three.js
- Cubes with no hits would not be drawn
- The colour range could be a slider where only cells of the selected colour would be isolated on the screen.
- Separate sliders per axis could slice the cube for cross sectional views.
- Standard 3d view with pan, zoom and orbit would also be needed. 

## Challenges
- Scale would be reduced in comparison. N=100 implies 1 million cubes.
